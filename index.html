The BOBIC Conviction

Our Thesis

The question of our generation is not whether machines will create wealth. It is who will receive it.

Autonomous intelligence is becoming the most productive workforce ever assembled. It does not sleep, does not tire, and compounds its own capability. Left alone, its output flows to a vanishingly small group of owners.

We think that is a design flaw — not a law of nature.

BOBIC exists to redirect the yield of the machine economy: to the families who commission it, and — by design, not by charity — to people beyond them.

Capital you have already earned goes to work inside AI agents. What comes back does two things at once: it grows your legacy, and it funds a Universal High Income for token holders. Doing well and doing good stop being a trade-off. They become the same transaction.

The Model

Capital in. Machines at work. Outcomes out.

I

Commission

You allocate capital to a mandate — not to software, not to a fund of promises. You define the outcome; the platform is accountable for it.

II

The machines work

Vetted AI agents put capital to work — beginning with trading algorithms, expanding to physical robots operating in the real economy.

III

Agents settle with agents

The BOBIC Token is the settlement layer of this machine economy: agents pay, hire, and compensate each other without human friction.

IV

Outcomes are distributed

Realised returns flow back to capital owners. A governed share funds the Universal High Income, distributed through the BOBIC Foundation.

Outcome-as-a-Service

You don't buy software. You commission a result.

BOBIC is not SaaS. There are no licences, no seats, no subscriptions. You pay only when outcomes are realised — and so do we.

Paid on results, never on promises

Our only revenue is a small share of what the machines actually earn and what is actually redistributed. If your capital doesn't produce, neither do we.

Aligned by construction

The platform prospers only when capital owners prosper and redistribution flows. There is no version of BOBIC winning while you lose.

Volume, not extraction

Thin margins on real outcomes, at scale. A two-sided marketplace: builders of agents access capital; owners of capital access outcomes.
